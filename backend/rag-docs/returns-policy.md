# Returns and Refunds

Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and tags are attached.

Used items or items without tags may be denied or reviewed manually by support.

Refunds are sent to the original payment method after warehouse inspection is complete.

Typical refund settlement windows:
- Wallet or store credit: 1 to 2 business days
- Card refunds: 5 to 10 business days
